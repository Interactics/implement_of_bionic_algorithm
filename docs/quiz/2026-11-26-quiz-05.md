# Quiz 05 · IK vs RL on the walks (2026-11-26)

Covers: [more-motions devlog, results](../devlog/2026-11-25-more-motions.md).

---

**Q1. Self-penetration drops from 10–31 % of frames (IK) to 0 (RL). What makes it disappear, given that the policy is never rewarded for avoiding it?**

> [!success]- Answer
> The simulator itself. The capsules collide, so the arm physically can't pass through the hip. The policy only learns to track as well as it can under that constraint.

**Q2. RL ground penetration is about 1 % of frames at about 1.4 cm, not exactly zero. Why can a simulated foot go into the floor at all?**

> [!success]- Answer
> MuJoCo uses soft contacts. A small penetration is how the contact force builds up, like a stiff spring. During heavy landings it briefly passes the 1 cm threshold.

**Q3. Foot sliding falls by 25–50 % but not to zero. Why does the paper accept this, too?**

> [!success]- Answer
> The reward tracks body poses, not the contact pattern. If shuffling a foot a little keeps the robot balanced and closer to the poses overall, the policy will do that. Forcing exact contacts would need an explicit contact term.

**Q4. 07_01 has 0 falls, but its mean helper force is 99 N. Why does that weaken the "physically feasible" claim, and how would you check it?**

> [!success]- Answer
> About 25 % of body weight comes from a force that doesn't exist on a real robot. To check, evaluate with the helper force switched off (or annealed to 0 during training) and see whether it still doesn't fall.
